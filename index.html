    What is TBRe

    Team Bath Racing Electric is the University of Bath's Formula Student team. Around 250 students from Engineering, Computer Science and Management design, build and race a fully electric single-seater, then compete against universities from around the world at Silverstone and Hockenheim.

    Everything on the car is designed in house. This year the technical team moves to four-wheel drive with in-hub motors, the biggest step TBRe has taken in years. TBReAI, the autonomous division, is targeting integration of its self-driving systems onto that same car. And Operations & Business keeps the whole thing running – brand design, marketing, sponsorships, and recruitment. You'll gain invaluable hands-on experience you can put on your CV and talk about in an interview.

    Don't stress

    Nobody joins TBRe knowing how to build a race car. Any course, any year, any level. Your first weeks are for finding out what the subteams do and picking what you want to learn. Someone on the team will onboard you, sit with you on your first tasks and answer whatever you ask.

    First years are actively wanted. The people who do the most on the car by their final year are the ones who turned up in their first.

    Questions people ask

    Can I join with no experience?

    Yes. We expect no prior knowledge. We're here to teach you and help you along, as long as you're proactive and motivated.

    I'm not doing an engineering degree. Can I still join?

    Yes. We have students from every course, from chemical engineering to biology to management. Come to the taster sessions, see what interests you, talk to the leads and we'll find the right role for you.

    How do I get involved?

    Come to the taster sessions and the weekly meetings, keep an eye on the WhatsApp for announcements, and put your hand up when the team needs help. The more we see you in lectures and lab tasks, the more we get to know you.

    How much time does it take?

    As much as you want to give. Some people do a few hours a week, others are in the lab most evenings. It ramps up towards competition in the summer.

    Is there a membership fee?

    No.

    Do I need to be in a subteam from day one?

    No. Tasters run through mid October, then you pick a subteam and onboarding starts.

    Can I join later in the year?

    Yes, but the earlier you join the more you learn and the more you get to do on the car.

    Can I drive the car?

    The car is driven by very experienced drivers who have contributed to the team over a long period of time. It's our pride and joy and needs someone we can trust in the cockpit. The best way to become a driver is not to ask.

    What's in it for me?

    Real engineering and business experience on a car that races at Silverstone and Hockenheim, sponsors who recruit from the team, and something concrete to talk about in interviews. Formula Student is the recognised gateway into F1 and motorsport, and TBRe alumni are across the paddock.
